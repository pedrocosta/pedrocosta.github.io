# HELLO, ZX SPECTRUM

The ZX Spectrum remains one of my favourite machines.

There is something wonderful about turning on a computer
and being presented with:

```text
Sinclair BASIC 48K

>

No desktop.
No login screen.
No updates.

Just a blinking cursor.

The first program
10 PRINT "HELLO, WORLD!"
20 GOTO 10

On a modern computer this is almost meaningless.

On an 8-bit machine with 48 KB of RAM,
it feels like magic.

Why BASIC?
BASIC made the machine approachable.

You could type something and immediately see the result.

That simplicity is something I want to preserve
in the projects documented here.

Sometimes limitations make computers more interesting.
